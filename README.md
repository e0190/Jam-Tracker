## ✨ The Vibe
Most dashboards just show the art. **E19** feels the music. Using a custom **Vibe-Based Color Extraction** algorithm, the entire UI shifts and glows to match the soul of your current track. No more muddy grays or flat blacks—just pure, vibrant energy that turns your monitor into a piece of digital decor.

---

## 🚀 Features
* **Vibe-Sync Technology:** Smarter color extraction that prioritizes vibrancy and saturation over "most used" pixels.
* **Dynamic Ambient Glow:** A breathing background effect that extends the album's energy across your entire screen.
* **Interactive Queue:** Real-time "Up Next" list with one-click track jumping.
* **Jam-Ready Integration:** Built-in QR code generator so friends can join your Spotify Jam session instantly.
* **Full-Screen Optimization:** Designed to look incredible on high-res monitors or dedicated secondary displays.
