## ✨ The Vibe
Most dashboards just show the art. **E19** feels the music. Using a custom Vibe-Based Color Extraction algorithm, the entire UI shifts and glows to match the soul of your current track. No more muddy grays—just pure, vibrant energy.

> **Insert a GIF here!** > Record your screen while skipping through 3-4 different songs (e.g., a bright pop song, a dark techno track, and a vibrant indie song) to show the background color-shifting in real-time.

## 🚀 Features
* **Vibe-Sync Technology:** Smarter color extraction that prioritizes vibrancy over "most used" pixels.
* **Dynamic Glow:** Subtle ambient lighting that breathes with the album art.
* **Interactive Queue:** See what's next and jump to any track with one click.
* **Jam-Ready:** Built-in QR code generator so friends can join your session instantly.
* **Fully Responsive:** Looks incredible on a 4K monitor or a dedicated tablet setup.
